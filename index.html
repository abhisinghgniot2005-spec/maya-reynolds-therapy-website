MEET YOUR THERAPIST

Warm, collaborative, grounded.

I’m a licensed clinical psychologist based in Santa Monica, California, offering therapy for adults who feel overwhelmed by anxiety, stress, or the lingering effects of past experiences.

My work integrates evidence-based methods including cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT), EMDR, mindfulness-based practices, and body-oriented techniques to explore both the emotional and physiological sides of what you’re experiencing.

I believe therapy works best when clients feel respected, understood, and actively involved. My goal is not only symptom relief, but greater insight, resilience, and a stronger relationship with yourself over time.

QUESTIONS

What you may want to know before getting started.

These answers reflect Dr. Reynolds’ practice details and approach.

Who does Dr. Reynolds work with?

She offers therapy for adults, including high-achieving professionals, entrepreneurs, and creatives who may be navigating anxiety, panic, trauma, burnout, perfectionism, or chronic stress.

Do you offer in-person and online therapy?

Yes. Dr. Reynolds offers in-person therapy from her Santa Monica office and secure telehealth sessions for clients located in California.

What therapy approaches do you use?

Her work integrates CBT, EMDR, mindfulness-based practices, and body-oriented techniques, with a warm, collaborative, grounded style.

What can trauma therapy focus on?

Trauma work may address single-incident trauma or more complex, long-standing patterns connected to childhood, relationships, or chronic stress, with careful pacing around safety and stabilization.

What is the office like?

The Santa Monica office is described as quiet, private, naturally lit, comfortable, and uncluttered—a space intended to help clients feel more at ease.
